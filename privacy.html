Privacy & Secure Communications

CyberAlex is a personal website. It is not a company website, customer portal, or commercial service.

This page explains the simple privacy position for this site and how to contact me more securely where needed.

Personal Website

CyberAlex is a personal website created and maintained by me, Alex. It exists to publish writing, tools, projects and related material.

It is not a company, not an online shop, not a managed service, and not a formal client platform. Nothing on this site should be interpreted as creating a business relationship, support obligation, or commercial service agreement unless explicitly stated elsewhere.

Personal Data Collection

No personal data is collected through the website itself.

CyberAlex does not provide account registration, user profiles, comment systems, contact forms, marketing sign ups, or other website features intended to collect personal information from visitors.

In normal use, you can browse the site without submitting personal data to me through the site itself.

Email and Communications

The contact page provides email addresses for getting in touch. Messages sent to the email address listed there should be assumed insecure for privacy purposes unless you are using Tuta.com email addresses.

Ordinary email can pass through multiple systems and should not be treated as private, confidential, or encrypted by default.

For private email communication, you should contact me using a Tuta.com email address, and send your message to my Tuta hosted address listed on the contact page.

Practical Guidance

Please use the following rule of thumb:

  • General enquiries: ordinary email is acceptable.
  • Private or sensitive matters: use a Tuta email address and contact my Tuta hosted address.

Tuta to Tuta email comminucation is private and secure.
